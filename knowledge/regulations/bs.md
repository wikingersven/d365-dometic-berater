# Electrical Regulations for Leisure Vehicles – UK/Ireland

> **Scope:** United Kingdom and Republic of Ireland
> **Language:** English
> **Base:** EN 1648-1/2 (adopted as BS EN 1648)

---

## 1. Applicable Standards

| Standard | Title | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| BS 7671 | IET Wiring Regulations (18th Edition) | Main UK wiring standard |
| BS EN 1648-1 | Habitable leisure vehicles – 12 V DC installations | 12 V system |
| BS EN 1648-2 | Habitable leisure vehicles – 230 V AC installations | 230 V system |
| IEC 60309 / CEE | Industrial plugs and sockets | Shore power (CEE blue, 16 A) |
| BS 7671 Section 721 | Caravans and motor caravans | Vehicle-specific requirements |
| Part P (England/Wales) | Building Regulations – Electrical Safety | Fixed installation context |

## 2. 230 V System – Key Points

- **RCD Protection:** Mandatory, 30 mA sensitivity, Type A or AC
- **MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker):** Required per circuit
- **Shore Power Inlet:** Blue CEE connector (IEC 60309), 16 A, IP44
- **UK Plug Note:** Internal UK 13 A sockets acceptable; shore power must use CEE blue
- **Cabling:** Flexible cables (H07RN-F or equivalent), minimum cross-section 1.5 mm²
- **No rigid cable:** Fixed installation cable (e.g., Twin & Earth) not suitable for vehicles
- **Earthing:** CPC continuous throughout, equipotential bonding to chassis
- **Priority Switching:** Recommended when inverter fitted (automatic mains/inverter changeover)
- **SELV:** Wet areas must use SELV only (≤ 50 V AC / ≤ 120 V DC)

## 3. 12 V System – Key Points

- **Cable Sizing:** Per BS EN 1648-1, maximum voltage drop < 5 %
- **Fusing:** Each circuit individually fused
- **Battery Isolator:** Recommended (main switch for leisure battery)
- **LiFePO4 Batteries:** BMS mandatory, charge parameters per manufacturer
- **Earth Return:** Star-point earthing recommended, chassis earth for starter circuit only
- **Polarity Marking:** Red = positive, blue/black = negative

## 4. System and Device Notes

- **Inverters:** CE/UKCA marking, pure sine wave recommended, earthing per BS 7671
- **Solar Systems:** String fusing when >2 parallel strings, reverse current diode
- **Chargers:** IUoU charging curve, LiFePO4 mode when lithium fitted
- **N-BUS System:** Cross-brand compatible (Büttner/NDS/Dometic)
- **NCC Verification:** Caravans and motorhomes may be subject to NCC Approved Scheme checks

## 5. Competent Person Requirements

- **230 V Installation:** Must be carried out by a competent/qualified electrician
- **Part P:** In England/Wales, notifiable electrical work in dwellings; vehicle installations generally exempt but best practice applies
- **12 V Installation:** Can be carried out by a competent person; qualified electrician recommended
- **Testing:** Initial verification required (BS 7671 Part 6), periodic inspection recommended
- **NCC Scheme:** New caravans/motorhomes checked under NCC Approved Dealer scheme

## 6. Agent Formulation Rules

- For 230 V queries, **always** mention requirement for qualified electrician
- Do not provide detailed 230 V installation instructions – product advice only
- For 12 V advice, remind about correct cable sizing and fuse protection
- Reference standards as BS 7671 / BS EN where applicable
- When in doubt, advise conservatively (larger cable, higher-rated protection)
- Note UKCA vs CE marking requirements post-Brexit where relevant
