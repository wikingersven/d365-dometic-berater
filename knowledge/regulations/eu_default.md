# Electrical Regulations – EU Default (HD 60364 / EN 1648)

> **Scope:** Fallback for EU/EEA countries without a dedicated regulation file
> **Language:** English
> **Base:** Harmonized HD 60364 and EN 1648-1/2

This file serves as the default regulatory reference when no country-specific regulation file exists. All EU member states implement HD 60364 through national standards. The EN 1648 series is harmonized across the EU and provides the specific requirements for leisure vehicle electrical systems.

---

## 1. Applicable Standards (Harmonized)

| Standard | Title | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| HD 60364 | Low-voltage electrical installations | Harmonized base standard (implemented nationally) |
| EN 1648-1 | Habitable leisure vehicles – 12 V DC installations | 12 V system |
| EN 1648-2 | Habitable leisure vehicles – 230 V AC installations | 230 V system |
| IEC 60309 / CEE | Industrial plugs and sockets | Shore power (CEE blue, 16 A) |
| HD 60364-7-721 | Caravans and motor caravans | Vehicle-specific section |
| EU LVD 2014/35/EU | Low Voltage Directive | CE marking for equipment |
| EU EMC 2014/30/EU | Electromagnetic Compatibility Directive | CE marking for equipment |

## 2. 230 V System – Key Points

- **RCD Protection:** Mandatory in all EU states, sensitivity ≤ 30 mA, Type A minimum
- **Circuit Breaker:** Required per individual circuit
- **Shore Power Inlet:** Blue CEE connector (IEC 60309), 16 A, IP44 – universal across EU
- **Cabling:** Flexible cables (H07RN-F or equivalent), minimum cross-section 1.5 mm²
- **No rigid cable:** Fixed installation cables are not permitted in vehicles
- **Earthing:** Protective conductor continuous throughout, equipotential bonding to chassis
- **Priority Switching:** Recommended when inverter installed
- **SELV:** Wet areas require SELV only (≤ 50 V AC / ≤ 120 V DC)
- **Mains Voltage:** 230 V ± 10 % single phase across EU (harmonized since HD 472 S1)

## 3. 12 V System – Key Points

- **Cable Sizing:** Per EN 1648-1, maximum voltage drop < 5 %
- **Fusing:** Each circuit individually fused
- **Battery Isolator:** Recommended (main switch for leisure battery)
- **LiFePO4 Batteries:** BMS mandatory, charge parameters per manufacturer specification
- **Earthing:** Star-point earthing recommended, chassis earth for starter circuit only
- **Polarity Marking:** Red = positive, blue/black = negative

## 4. System and Device Notes

- **Inverters:** CE marking mandatory (EU market), pure sine wave recommended
- **Solar Systems:** String fusing when >2 parallel strings, reverse current diode
- **Chargers:** IUoU charging curve, LiFePO4 mode when lithium battery fitted
- **N-BUS System:** Cross-brand compatible (Büttner/NDS/Dometic)
- **CE Marking:** All electrical equipment must carry CE marking for EU market

## 5. Competent Person Requirements

- **230 V Installation:** Must be carried out by a qualified electrician (national requirements vary)
- **12 V Installation:** Can be carried out by a competent person; qualified electrician recommended
- **Testing:** Initial verification required, periodic inspection recommended
- **National Variations:** Always check the specific national implementation of HD 60364
- **Note:** When a customer's country has a dedicated regulation file, use that instead

## 6. Agent Formulation Rules

- For 230 V queries, **always** mention requirement for qualified electrician
- Do not provide detailed 230 V installation instructions – product advice only
- For 12 V advice, remind about correct cable sizing and fuse protection
- Reference harmonized EN standards; note that national implementations may vary
- When in doubt, advise conservatively (larger cable sizes, higher-rated protection)
- Recommend checking with a local qualified electrician for country-specific requirements
- If customer's country is identified, check if a specific regulation file exists first
- Use Dometic branding for international markets unless NDS (IT/Benelux) or Büttner (DACH)
