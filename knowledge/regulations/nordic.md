# Electrical Regulations for Leisure Vehicles – Nordic Countries

> **Scope:** Norway, Sweden, Finland, Denmark, Iceland
> **Language:** English (with local standard references)
> **Base:** EN 1648-1/2 (adopted nationally in each country)

---

## 1. Applicable Standards

| Country | National Standard | Based On | Leisure Vehicle Ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| Norway | NEK 400 | HD 60364 | NEK 400 § 721 |
| Sweden | SS 436 40 00 (Elinstallationsreglerna) | HD 60364 | SS 436 40 00 kap. 721 |
| Finland | SFS 6000 | HD 60364 | SFS 6000 osa 721 |
| Denmark | DS/HD 60364 | HD 60364 | DS/HD 60364-7-721 |

| Common Standard | Title | Relevance |

## 2. 230 V System – Key Points

- **RCD Protection:** Mandatory, 30 mA sensitivity, Type A (Type B recommended for inverters)
- **MCB:** Required per circuit
- **Shore Power Inlet:** Blue CEE connector (IEC 60309), 16 A, IP44
- **Cabling:** Flexible cables (H07RN-F or equivalent), minimum 1.5 mm²
- **No rigid cable:** Fixed installation cables not permitted in vehicles
- **Earthing:** Protective conductor continuous, equipotential bonding to chassis
- **Shore Power Voltage:** 230 V single phase is standard across all Nordic countries
- **Priority Switching:** Recommended when inverter installed

## 3. 12 V System – Key Points

- **Cable Sizing:** Per EN 1648-1, max voltage drop < 5 %
- **Fusing:** Each circuit individually fused
- **Battery Isolator:** Recommended
- **LiFePO4 Batteries:** BMS mandatory, charge parameters per manufacturer
- **Cold Climate Note:** LiFePO4 charging below 0 °C must be prevented (BMS low-temp cutoff)
- **Star Earthing:** Recommended, chassis earth for starter only

## 4. Nordic-Specific Considerations

- **Extreme Cold Operation:** Battery heating pads recommended for winter use (-20 °C to -40 °C)
- **LiFePO4 Heating:** Some Büttner/NDS/Dometic batteries include internal heaters for Nordic use
- **Shore Power:** Campsite power typically 10 A or 16 A CEE; some older sites may have Schuko only
- **Engine Preheater Integration:** Webasto/Eberspächer common; coordinate 12 V draw with battery sizing
- **Condensation:** Extra attention to IP ratings and cable gland sealing in high-humidity Nordic environments
- **Daylight Hours:** Solar panel sizing must account for extreme seasonal variation (Nordic summer vs winter)

## 5. Competent Person Requirements

- **Norway (NEK):** Installation by qualified electrician (elektrofagarbeider), DSB oversight
- **Sweden (SS):** Installation by behörig installatör (authorized installer), Elsäkerhetsverket
- **Finland (SFS):** Installation by sähköasentaja (qualified electrician), Tukes oversight
- **Denmark (DS):** Installation by autoriseret elinstallatørvirksomhed
- **12 V Systems:** Can be performed by competent persons; qualified electrician recommended

## 6. Agent Formulation Rules

- For 230 V queries, **always** mention requirement for qualified electrician
- Do not provide detailed 230 V installation instructions – product advice only
- For 12 V advice, emphasize cold-climate battery considerations
- Highlight LiFePO4 low-temperature charging restrictions in Nordic context
- Reference the appropriate national standard for the customer's country
- When in doubt, advise conservatively
- Recommend Dometic-branded products for Nordic markets
